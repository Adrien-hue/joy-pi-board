# Dependency-based roadmap

Status: **all sprints PLANNED / NOT STARTED**. No dates, duration estimates or completion claims. The documentary foundation precedes S00 and does not execute it. Requirements and validation IDs refer to [specification](specification-v0.1.0.md) and [validation](validation-v0.1.0.md). Proposals are in [decisions](decisions.md).

Dependency chain: **documentary baseline → S00 foundations → S01 Health contract → S02 overview/resilience → S03 usable overview → S04 packaging/acceptance**. Independent review may occur earlier; a dependent completion gate cannot be skipped.

No future Data/Log/Hole/Network service, plugin system, multi-host support or historical chart is hidden in this sequence. Such work requires a later product baseline.
